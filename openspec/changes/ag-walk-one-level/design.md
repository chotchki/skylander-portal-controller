## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**
- A kid's character walking chapter 1's opening on the Mac with an 8BitDo pad, moving the way the original moves by every check the oracle can make.
- Every behaviour AG writes from observation of a script is a spec requirement AJ's VM must pass before it deletes the stand-in, all of them in one list.
- The oracle as a per-frame recorder, so the movement and camera specs and the model replay stand on traces of the original, not on the decompile.

**Non-Goals:**
- Anything scripted past the stand-ins: enemies, NPCs, critters, breakables, Titan feats, tutorial prompts, the intro cinematic (`213_Intro_IGC`) and the HUD (AH, AJ, AM).
- Rigid-body physics. Nothing in the tutorial's live world is dynamic.
- Co-op (AL). One hero; a second character waits.
- What the collision materials mean (ag-motion's open question 4). Plain ground needs none of it; the IR keeps each triangle's material so AJ's surface handlers can read it.
- Music, ambience and footsteps (decision 16).
- Any level but 213; the hub and the PvP arenas (AI's).
- The Home menu (design doc Open question 1) and AP.7's directional moves, still the backlog's.
- Windows; the couch (the TV pass in [`openspec/backlog.md`](../../backlog.md)).
- Leans, random idles and footstep tags (AJ's locomotion scripts).

## Decisions

**1. Chapter 1's opening, Eruptor checked end to end, every modelled character walking on its own tuning.**
Level 213 because every oracle state, divergence number and camera check so far is Eruptor in it, AH's first fight is in it, and it IS chapter 1: `init_setup.bld` orders the story adventures with `Level_213_Tutorial` first and `global.bld` pairs it with "Time of the Giants" (measured, ag-motion section 4), and AC.13's session log opens `level/level_213_tutotial.bld` at 0:00:54.6. What a kid can reach there without scripts (the brief's arithmetic, [`reach-l213.txt`](/Users/chotchki/workspace/guacamole-research/ag-propose/reach-l213.txt)): 1,235 of the 3,080 collision triangles are walkable (normal within 45° of up), 24,730 m²; the patch joined to the spawn by shared edges is 143 triangles (1,658 m²) and closing gaps up to 0.3 m adds nothing; one-way drops add four patches (7.5 m down to 1,308 m², 23.8 m down to 645 m²), 3,658 m² in all. Placement solids aren't in that sketch: the gate's breakable door (12 m from the spawn) and `Breakable_GateRubble_Wall(1)` at the patch's far edge close it, so "the opening area" is the claim and AG.5 restates the number. A level with more open ground (the hub, an arena) would walk further but carry no oracle states.

Characters: every one with an AE model walks, each on its own tuning, read statically from the links its setup scripts pass (speed level into `Speed_VerySlow`-`VeryFast`, 20-25 ft/s, with the boot tuning raising `Speed_Slow` to the 21.5 AF.9 measured; turn class into `TurnRate_Fast`-`Titan`, 1440 down to 360°/s; air speed set to ground speed, air deceleration 50; default scale). Checked on two characters' live reads: Eruptor (21.5 ft/s, 1440°/s, a sphere) and Tree Rex, a Giant whose disc values (25 ft/s, 1440°/s) the scripts turn into 20 and 360 at run time, on a capsule. If the static links don't reproduce both live reads, the fallback is Eruptor alone, everyone else keeping their turntable card. Kids place their own figures, and a walk that refuses most of them reads broken. Rejected: waiting for AJ (M2 would be one hero for three rungs for no gain in correctness). Open question 1.

**3. Physics: Avian for queries only, behind a seam with a Parry twin.**
- The dependency: avian3d 0.8 from crates.io; until it publishes, Avian's `main` at `1070759b2145` ("Update to Bevy 0.20 (#1084)") as a git dependency pinned by rev, never #1070's `34c9b008d2f4`. Read on 2026-10-18: #1070 closed unmerged, the maintainer writing "Done in #1084, which is based on this but fixes CI and bumps the dependencies to released versions. I merged it to main already, and will try to get the actual Avian 0.8 release done tomorrow"; `main` builds avian3d `0.8.0-dev` on Bevy 0.20, parry3d 0.31 and `bevy_transform_interpolation` 0.6; crates.io's newest is 0.7.0, on Bevy 0.19. chotchki's pick stands; the branch the developer recommends moved.
- What's used, all on `main` at that rev: `SpatialQuery::{cast_shape, cast_ray, shape_intersections, project_point}`, `contact_manifolds`, `Collider::trimesh_with_config` with `TrimeshFlags::FIX_INTERNAL_EDGES` (which replaces the original's welding words), static colliders for the level and kinematic or sensor colliders for placements. No `RigidBody::Dynamic` anywhere: the live tutorial's `hkpWorld` holds one static body, 57 shape phantoms and 9 AABB phantoms, and no dynamic body (ag-motion verdict 3).
- The seam lives in a new pure crate, `crates/guac-motion` (no Bevy): a shape cast returning the time of impact and the contact planes there, contact manifolds within a distance at a pose, a ray cast returning the triangle it hit (so its material), overlaps. Two implementations: Avian's in `guacamole` (an adapter over `SpatialQuery` inside the movement system) and parry3d's in `guac-motion` over static trimeshes and convex primitives, which runs every controller test without Bevy and is the fallback if Avian's schedule or API bites, since nothing needs a solver.
- Rejected: Avian's solver and Rapier (no dynamics to solve); a MOPP reader (Parry builds its own tree from the explicit triangles, ag-levels).
- Cost: Avian and Parry join the `guacamole` binary's build and so `.githooks/pre-push`; AG.3 measures it cold and warm, as AA.10 did for Bevy.

**4. The controller: the original's loop, written from the spec, on the seam.**
`guac-motion` implements the hero-movement spec on the seam in the original's shape (ag-motion section 1): the velocity model (facing toward the stick, turning scaled by the stick's strength squared and the angle left, top speed shrinking while turning, the heading gate of 135° moving, 15° from rest and 45° without input, deceleration always and acceleration on top, a soft speed cap, gravity to terminal); the proxy loop (at most 10 casts a step, a manifold that drops near-duplicate planes, a frictionless plane solve, no upward gain); support by a downward probe at 45°; the snap to 0.01 m above ground within 0.375 m; tilt toward the ground at up to 120°/s; the downhill hug rays (from 2 m up, 4 m long) and the ground finder. Shapes come from `CollisionInfo`: full extents in feet times the scale, 0.1 m smaller all round with a 0.05 m convex radius, the pivot at the bottom centre, checked exactly against the three shapes ag-motion verified live (a push block, Norticus, a sheep) and Eruptor's (a sphere of radius 0.864 m, its centre 0.914 m up at his 150 %).

Why this over a built-in: what decides the oracle's branch outcomes (which planes count as one, how the solve slides, that nothing gains height) is known and small, and the model replay (decision 7) only compares like with like if the loop is the original's. AG.6 writes the requirements from per-frame traces, the decompile only explaining them, and the acceleration shows why. AF.9's first 30 flips from rest covered 2.97-2.99 m; the model as read (acceleration minus deceleration, 80 ft/s² net) predicts 2.74-2.82 m given that run's long first frame, and acceleration alone 3.00-3.05 m ([`accel.txt`](/Users/chotchki/workspace/guacamole-research/ag-propose/accel.txt)). Neither fits exactly (the initial 15° turn and the integration order aren't modelled), and the reading sits furthest out. Rejected: Avian's `MoveAndSlide` with its per-hit callback (iterations, depenetration, a 0.01 skin and up to 20 similar planes; the callback can express no upward gain, but its solve and plane merging aren't the original's, so a branch could go the other way for reasons nobody can name; worth a measured comparison only if cheap); tnua or ahoy (someone else's feel).

**5. Time: a fixed 1/60 s step, and what "the original tick" means.**
The original integrates its measured frame time, clamped to between half and three times the nominal 1/60 s, so [1/120, 1/20] s (ag-motion section 1). At full speed that's 1/60 s and its long frames are hitches, not design: AF.9's steady 30-flip legs covered 3.40-3.42 m, which is 29 flips at 1/60 s plus one 0.036 s flip after each `PAUSE_AT` at 6.553 m/s (3.403 m, [`accel.txt`](/Users/chotchki/workspace/guacamole-research/ag-propose/accel.txt)). guacamole steps the hero and the camera in `FixedUpdate` at 60 Hz with dt = 1/60 s, transforms interpolated for display (`bevy_transform_interpolation`, which comes with Avian). The design doc's "the logic stays at the original tick" then means the nominal 1/60 s; AG.18 writes that down as a definition, not a new departure. Every comparison keys on accumulated game time, never on flips. Rejected: a variable step (it inherits the host's hitches and no test repeats); stepping at the recording's dt outside a replay (meaningless without one; the model replay feeds the recorded dt to the controller directly).

**6. O5: a per-frame recorder that doesn't pause.**
AF.11 showed that observing moves the run, and every `PAUSE_AT` injects one long frame. O5 is P9 in AF decision 2's form: game-agnostic C++, Giants' addresses in our Rust. `WATCH <frames> <address> <length> ...` arms a ring; once per flip the emulator copies the named ranges into it at the guest's pad read, where O4 already keys the flip under the pad mutex, so every sample is taken at the same point of the game's frame, after that frame's pad read and on the guest thread doing it. `WATCH_READ` returns the ring at a pause and `WATCH_CLEAR` drops it; ranges times frames are bounded. The harness names what to watch from AF.9's layout: the hero's corpus (transform, velocity) and placement (position and angles), `tfbPS3Application`'s `_frameDelta` and `_curFrame`, the camera's viewport (placement, FOV), the pad readback and, for decision 14, the hero's current clip and its time. That the game updates actors on the thread that reads the pad is a hypothesis; AG.1's check (each position step equal to velocity times `_frameDelta` on open ground) tests it. Rejected: `PAUSE_AT` checkpoints (they move the run); a guest dump per frame (far too slow, and PINE reads race a running game).

**7. Feel against the oracle: four layers, frames not one of them.**
Remaster only: "parity" means gameplay, never pixels (design doc, Decisions' fifth batch).

| Layer | What's compared | Tolerance | Why it's possible |
|---|---|---|---|
| Exact | the hero's start pose; the camera's rest pose relative to him (55 ft at 32°, on its heading); the extracted collision mesh against the live `hkpWorld` body | float noise (1e-3 m, 1e-3°) | AF.11: at-rest observables are bit-exact |
| Model replay | `guac-motion` fed ONE recording's per-frame dt and stick from its own F0 pose, then per-frame position, velocity and heading | set from the first run and justified in AG.11's box; expected near-exact on open ground, looser after a contact | the oracle's nondeterminism is in dt and timing, which the recording carries |
| Envelope | scalar feel metrics at guacamole's fixed step, keyed on game time: top speed, time to 90 % of it, stop distance, the 180° reversal (stop, turn, go), turn rate, wall-slide speed, the camera's trailing angle at top speed, the zone switch's timing | the oracle replays' spread at AF.12's floors and k | none of these integrates host time |
| Outcome | at a branch (the gate's corner), guacamole's outcome is one the oracle produced | set membership | AF.11: 15 of 20 replays slid along it, 5 turned free |
| Not compared | frames (FLIP recorded once for the record, AE.16's rule); RNG | none | the remaster; the walk's draws are ambient (birds, leaves), none decides where the hero goes |

**8. IR v3 and the level stage.**
IR v3 and `LAYOUT_VERSION` 3 add a level stage after AE's models and before ffmpeg is looked for, so a machine without ffmpeg still walks (only the intros wait on it). Per level it writes: world-space segments on AE's segment and material records (no transforms to apply: the static scenes have none, ag-levels); textures into AE's `textures/<hash>.ktx2` store, deduplicated against the characters'; collision as world-space vertices (the body's translation applied, its rotation asserted identity), triangles and one material per triangle, the welding words dropped and the MOPP skipped; placements (id, name, transform, scale, model through AE's model record, solid shape and bits, `ActorParameters`, waypoint lists, activity state); lights; the sky's groups; AG.8's script data. The manifest names the levels it holds, each with its own completion, so a later rung that plays another level adds a stage entry that resumes instead of rebuilding; anything else follows AB's rules. The readers refuse what ag-levels never saw (another shape class, a second body or system, a rotated body, an index out of range, a count mismatch) by name, and tfbmulti2 materials with bit 6 or 9 the way AE does. Size and time get measured (AG.9); level 213's `level.bld` alone is 110 MB with 70,839 objects.

**9. The level's look.**
- AE's shader draws the 857 tfbmulti2 instances of the tutorial's 912 (the census, tallied by the brief). Past what a hero uses they need the gloss map on 1 material and the scene bits AE turns off on the turntable: fog on all 857, point lights on 838 (the level's one point light, `213_EvilRobot_Beam_light`) and shadow receive on 494. The gloss term goes into the character-shading spec (from ae-shader's model, in our words) and into AE.7's reference model with tests (AE decision 10's method). The gloss texture is a weight, used as stored the way tints are, not decoded as a colour. Bits 6 and 9 appear on no tfbmulti2 material here and stay refused.
- Lights: `_worldLight1`-`3` are the three directional lights the material reads; `_causticLight`'s role is unknown and AG draws without it, recording what it is if AG.7's capture shows.
- Shadows: Bevy's cascaded shadow map of `_worldLight1` feeds the material's shadow-receive term (the term is twice a shadow sample's mix by the scene's shadow mode, character-shading's order of terms); how a Bevy shadow visibility maps onto that sample is set in AG.12 and recorded there. Rejected: reading the game's own shadow pass (its map is a render target, zero in a capture, so it means reading the pass's programs and matching it, for a look that departs by design anyway); no shadows (the level looks flat, and Bevy's cascades are where the Enhanced line starts).
- Stand-ins, each listed in AG.12's box: grass (`0xaa6`, the 45 grass segments, hypothesis tfbgrass) drawn with its colour map alpha-tested and two-sided, no wind; warp (`tfbwarp`, 4) as a blended surface with its colour map, no distortion; the 6 instances of `0xaa8` named first through `global.bld`'s effect exports as AE.1 named `0xaa7` and `0xaa9` (hypothesis tfbshadow), and not drawn if they're the shadow pass's, which Bevy's cascades replace.
- The sky's seven groups draw as their materials say. Culling is Bevy's per-entity frustum culling in place of the `igSpatialTree`; the disc has no LOD data.
- Every level material key is compiled before the level first shows, through AE.14's pre-warm, because macOS compiles a pipeline inside the frame that first needs it. The budget is AE.17's floor: about 433,000 triangles (395,214 in scene 0, 37,740 grass) and 857 instances.

**10. Placements: what exists at the start, and what's solid.**
- What exists: AG.5 sets the rule from AF.9's live actor list and the fresh state's screenshot against level 213's 997 placements. The hypothesis is that activity state 5 marks a template a script clones (`Push_Block_Template(1)`, `Fence Spawner_Template(1)` and `Titan_Floor(Template)(1)` are among the 413) and 4 a placement present from the start (584); nothing pins it yet.
- What's solid: a static prop present at the start gets its `CollisionInfo` solid (decision 4's shapes) when its solid bit is set. Non-solid volumes are inert sensors, except the camera zones (decision 11) and whatever AG.6 finds a fall volume does (decision 15).
- Scripted creatures (anything skinned with a script: enemies, NPCs, critters) are left out with their solids until their scripts run (Open question 3). Shown idling with colliders instead, frozen Chompies and a silent Norticus invite "why don't they fight".
- The gate's door: `Breakable_Stone_Wall_Castle_206(1)` (4 × 21 × 34 ft at heading 180°, 12 m from the spawn) fills the 6.6 m gateway between the static walls at x ≈ 10.5 and 17.1 only if its length runs along x, not along z as ag-motion's table reads its box ([`gateway-walls.txt`](/Users/chotchki/workspace/guacamole-research/ag-propose/gateway-walls.txt)), so a box's axes are a hypothesis until AG.5 checks the door model's bounds and one oracle contact. Which collider makes AF.11's gate corner is AG.6's.

**11. The camera stand-in.**
A native follow from the camera system's own data (AG.8): the camera definition (look-from distance, pitch and heading, absolute or relative; look-at offset; FOV; far clip), a "magic point" easing toward the hero at the system's speed and easing (`cameraSpeed` 0.4, `PanEasing` 0.01, `ZoomEasing` 0.05 and the rest of `Camera - System - Init`'s defaults), the framing zoom between its bounds (one target in AG), zones polled every 0.08 s that switch to the zone's camera over the time the zone carries (its X rotation, in seconds) with the system's ease, and transition planes with a camera on each side. No camera collision, because the original has none: no camera macro casts a ray (ag-motion section 3). The constants are data AG.8 reads; the law is ours, written from AG.7's recordings into the gameplay-camera spec. Checked exactly at rest, inside the envelope while trailing, and by timing at a zone switch. Two numbers stay open until AG.7 reads them in play: a 0.8° elevation gap ag-motion found on the June pause state, and a live FOV that read 111 there.

**12. Pad input and prompts.**
- A level layer in AP's router; no action-mapping crate (AP's call carries: the router is small). While the level shows it takes the pad, and an intro playing over it still takes any button as its skip (character-intros).
- The move stick reads raw: Bevy's own dead zone is turned off for the left stick (the exact `GamepadSettings` field recorded in AG.13), then the original's rule, measured in code and to be swept in AG.6: per axis, under a quarter is zero and the rest rescales from a quarter to full; the strength is the length of the two, capped at 1; the direction is the stick's angle plus the active camera's heading (AF.9: forward walks along the camera's heading, left at its +90°).
- Buttons by position: South, East, West, North, Start pausing under a one-button overlay, Select and Home doing nothing (Home waits on the design doc's Open question 1). No button jumps (no hero jumps in Giants: `jumpInitialSpeed` is 0 on every hero, ag-motion section 5; AF.11's walk script labels South "jump", which is its label, not the game's).
- Prompts: a resolver from a pad's vendor and product id to a label set (Xbox for the PC variant, Nintendo for the Switch variant), and for any pad it can't place, the button's position drawn as a glyph, which claims no label it can't back and needs no setting; the phone's glyph-set choice stays a candidate in the backlog's settings line. The table's ids come from the Mac half of the backlog's controller hardware checks, which moves into AG.13. AG draws prompts only on its own overlays (pause, the place-a-Skylander message): the original's prompts are tutorial scripts (AJ).

**13. Portal and level, and intros in gameplay.**
- Entering: the first character placed takes the TV into the level after its intro, as the hero at the start (Open question 4; the original's flow minus its story menus). Magic items, adventure-pack pieces and sidekicks never start the level or become the hero.
- A second character placed waits on the portal (co-op is AL). Removing the hero pauses the level under a "place a Skylander" message, unless another character waits; the next hero comes in where AG.4 sees the original bring one in (hypothesis: at the old hero's position) and the level carries on. The level lasts while Giants is the current game: there's no menu to leave it before AM's story menus.
- The TV: the level replaces the portal view's cards (the figure-cards delta) and keeps the small join code (AA's join requirement, unchanged). AE.13's per-slot turntables stop rendering while the level shows; whichever of AE.13 and AG.15 lands second keeps the other's tests passing.
- Intros: the backlog's "Character intros in gameplay" line, promoted. Measured so far: AC.13's session loaded Eruptor at chapter 1's "put a Skylander on the Portal" prompt, on an existing save with a `.sky` used before, and the only `.mov` files opened all session were the two boot logos ([`RPCS3.log`](/Users/chotchki/workspace/guacamole-research/captures/BLUS30968/09d602fd5/session-20261018/RPCS3.log)); AF.11's in-level `LOAD`s of a fresh `.sky` copy opened none either. So the intros delta says now: none once the level has started, paused or not; the portal view, guacamole's own screen before any level, keeps M0's rule. Unmeasured: a first-ever placement on a new save, a never-placed figure mid-level, per save or per toy.
