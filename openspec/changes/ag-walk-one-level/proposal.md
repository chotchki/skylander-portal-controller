# Phase AG - M2: the figure on the portal walks the opening of chapter 1

## Why

M2 is the first gameplay: put a character on the phone and walk it with a pad through a real Giants level on the TV, moving, turning, sliding and falling the way the original does. It stands on AE (the model, its material and the remaster) and on AF (an oracle that can be driven frame-locked, with its drift from itself measured). The 2026-10-18 research closed what a level is on the disc and how the hero moves, so the rung can be planned from evidence rather than guesses; the synthesis is [`ag-propose/BRIEF.md`](/Users/chotchki/workspace/guacamole-research/ag-propose/BRIEF.md), standing on [`ag-levels`](/Users/chotchki/workspace/guacamole-research/ag-levels/FINDINGS.md) and [`ag-motion`](/Users/chotchki/workspace/guacamole-research/ag-motion/FINDINGS.md):

- A level is one IGZ, its static geometry already in world space (16,912 segments over the disc's 75 scenes, 0 decode errors), its collision ONE explicit triangle mesh per world (68 bodies, 140,603 triangles) and everything else a placement. No trigger, camera-path or navmesh type exists on the disc: that's all script.
- Havok is query-only. The tutorial's live `hkpWorld` holds no dynamic body, and the hero moves by Toys for Bob's own kinematic velocity model over their fork of Havok's character proxy. So AG needs shape casts, contact planes and rays, not a solver.
- The camera, the hero's runtime speed and turn rate, his locomotion clips, the spawn, fog and death falls are tfbScript, and the VM is AJ, three rungs up. AG writes stand-ins for what it needs from oracle observation, as specs AJ's VM must later pass.
- AF.11 (on `af-divergence`, `dd571bf`) says what "feel checked against the oracle" can't mean: trajectory equality. A still hero and camera are bit-exact for 7,200 flips, but a walking hero parts within 60, the gate's corner splits replays into two outcomes that end 1.3-1.7 m apart (an end spread of 1.48 m in the unpaused set, 1.24 m in the paused one), and pausing to observe moves the run.

## What Changes

- **The level is chapter 1, `level/level_213_tutotial.bld` ("Time of the Giants"), from its spawn.** Every oracle state so far stands there (AF's fresh state idles Eruptor at the spawn) and AH's first fight is in it. With no scripts running, the static collision joined to the spawn is about 1,660 m², 3,660 m² with one-way drops, 7-15 % of the level's walkable collision, closed off by a breakable door and a rubble wall that are AH's and AJ's. With the props that stand there at the start solid too, the hero reaches 703 m² (AG.7: the clearing, the way past the bridge's foot and the sheep's ground), closed off by breakables, the gate's door and barrels and a pot on the ledge route, that attacks open (AH). So M2 honestly reads "walk the opening area of Time of the Giants", not the whole level (Open question 3).
- **Who walks**: Eruptor first, on his live numbers, checked end to end against the oracle; then every character with an AE model on its own tuning, read statically and checked on Eruptor and Tree Rex (Open question 1).
- **The first walk comes early.** A thin slice (AG.21) puts Eruptor in the level on the TV, walked by a pad on the extracted collision with camera 0 following, as soon as the level, the controller, the camera and the input layer exist, and chotchki looks at it before the envelope work and before his answer on the stand-ins is needed.
- **Physics: queries only, our controller.** Avian behind a thin seam (chotchki: "probably avian if I had to guess"): avian3d 0.8 from crates.io, or `main` at `1070759b2145` ("Update to Bevy 0.20 (#1084)") pinned by rev until 0.8 publishes. The design doc's pin, avianphysics/avian#1070 at `34c9b008d2f4`, is stale: #1070 closed unmerged on 2026-10-18 and #1084, built on it, merged to `main` instead. A parry3d twin of the seam runs every controller test without Bevy, and both sides are tested headless and replayed against the oracle. The hero is moved by a controller written from the hero-movement spec, every mechanism observed in the oracle first, the decompile only explaining it.
- **Script stand-ins until AJ**: static readers of the data the scripts pass as macro arguments (camera definitions, volumes and transition planes, tuning links, locomotion states, fog zones) plus native logic written from oracle observation. Each is a requirement in observable terms, listed in one place with the rung that deletes it. Reading macro arguments and replaying their bodies is the narrow rewrite the Method rules out without chotchki's yes, as AE's toy table was ("that's fine"), and this one is several times its size (Open question 2, which blocks only the readers and what reads them).
- **A per-frame oracle recorder that doesn't pause**: O5, a game-agnostic `WATCH` verb in AF's dev-only O-series, copying address ranges the harness names into a ring once per flip at the flip's first pad read. It feeds the model replay (each frame's step), the movement and camera specs and AH after.
- **Feel checked in four layers, frames not one of them** (remaster only: "parity" means gameplay, never pixels): exact rest poses, top speed and collision; a model replay of oracle recordings' own steps, stick and view, its tolerance declared before it runs; scalar envelope metrics (top speed, ramp, stop distance, reversal, turn rate, wall slide, camera trail) over pause-free replays, each floor built from named terms by AF.12's method; the gate corner's outcomes reproduced by the replay.
- **Time**: logic in `FixedUpdate` at 1/60 s with interpolated transforms. The original integrates its measured frame time clamped to [1/120, 1/20] s, so "the original tick" in the design doc is a nominal 1/60 s and its long frames are hitches, not design; every comparison keys on game time, the sum of those clamped steps.
- **The level on the TV in the remaster's look**: AE's material on level 213's 857 tfbmulti2 instances plus the gloss term one of them needs, fog, point lights and shadows from Bevy's cascades of the level's key light; stand-ins for grass (45 instances), warp (4) and an unidentified effect (6); the sky as authored; the level loading in the background from the Giants pick, every material compiled before it first shows.
- **Pad input**: buttons by position, the original's stick dead zone, the stick read against the camera's view, one pad walking the hero, Start pausing, a lost pad pausing; prompts by the label printed on the pad in hand, with a position glyph for a pad guacamole can't identify. The Mac half of the backlog's controller hardware checks runs first, on any build (Open question 5 only if the two 8BitDo variants look alike).
- **Portal and level**: the first character placed takes the TV into the level after its intro (Open question 4), a second waits, removing the hero pauses under a "place a Skylander" overlay, other figures change nothing, and the level replaces the cards on the TV. The backlog's "Character intros in gameplay" line is promoted here: in the two cases measured so far the original played no intro (AC.13's placement at the chapter's prompt, AF.7's mid-level), which the intros delta carries now; AG.6 measures a figure's first-ever placement and the rest.
- **The extract grows**: IR v3 (v4 since the AE/AG merge: AE.11 took v3 in parallel) and layout v4 (AE.6 took layout 3 for the models) add a level stage after AE's models (world-space segments on AE's records, prop models, textures into AE's store, collision, placements, lights, sky; the stand-ins' script data later, as a part of its own) and a hero side (each character's locomotion clips, shape and disc movement parameters).
- Out: enemies, attacks, NPCs and other scripted creatures, the intro cinematic, the HUD, tutorial prompts, breakables, Titan feats, falling out of the level (none reachable), music and effects (AH, AJ, AM); co-op (AL); Windows (the bring-up line).

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `guacamole/level-scene`: the level guacamole plays and how it gets there: chapter 1's opening off the user's disc, what's in it at the start, what's solid, its look, loading it without a stall, and how the portal takes the TV into it and keeps it there.
- `guacamole/hero-movement`: how a hero moves under the stick, as the oracle shows the original: camera-relative walking, top speed, speeding up and stopping, turning and hard reversals, walls, slopes, steps and drops, leaning with the ground, falling, no jumping, his locomotion animation and frame-rate independence.
- `guacamole/gameplay-camera`: where the camera starts, how it follows, how the level's transition planes and volumes switch it and that nothing pushes it in.
- `guacamole/pad-input`: buttons by position, the move stick's dead zone, which pad walks the hero, prompts that name the button printed on the pad in hand, and Start's pause.

### Modified Capabilities

- `guacamole/character-intros` (archived base in [`openspec/specs/guacamole/`](../../specs/guacamole/character-intros/spec.md)): "Placing a figure plays its character's intro" gets the level case; "Intros queue, skip and stop" and "Intros wait for extraction" scoped to the level (back-to-back intros before the start end in the level, and none plays once it has started). AG.6 rewrites them from the full measurement.
- `guacamole/disc-extraction` (archived base): one ADDED requirement, levels extracted after the figures. ADDED, not MODIFIED, since AE's delta already modifies "Extraction runs once and survives interruption".
- `guacamole/figure-cards` (AA's requirements, AE's MODIFIED version of one, all unarchived): "Each figure on the portal shows as a card" and "Cards follow the portal" hold on the portal view, not over a level.
- `guacamole/figure-turntable` (AE's new capability, unarchived): "A placed character turns on its card", "The turntable never stalls the TV" and "Eight turning figures fit" scoped to the portal view.
- `guacamole/character-shading` (AE's new capability, unarchived): one ADDED requirement, the gloss map.

The figure-cards, figure-turntable and character-shading deltas sit on unarchived changes, so `openspec validate` passes now and AG archives only after AA and AE, its MODIFIED blocks rebuilt from their archived text first (design, Migration Plan).
