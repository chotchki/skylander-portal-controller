# Phase AH - M3: the first fight, earned and kept on the kid's profile

## Why

M3 is the first fight: a kid's character attacks, takes hits, beats the enemies the original puts in its way and keeps the experience and gold it earns on that kid's profile, which the phone then shows and edits. It stands on AG (a hero walking chapter 1 on the TV) and AF (an oracle that can be driven frame-locked, its drift measured). The 2026-10-19 research closed where the first fight is, what it computes and how much of it is script, so the rung can be planned from evidence; the synthesis is [`ah-propose/BRIEF.md`](/Users/chotchki/workspace/guacamole-research/ah-propose/BRIEF.md), standing on [`ah-combat`](/Users/chotchki/workspace/guacamole-research/ah-combat/FINDINGS.md), [`ah-progression`](/Users/chotchki/workspace/guacamole-research/ah-progression/FINDINGS.md), [`aj-vm`](/Users/chotchki/workspace/guacamole-research/aj-vm/FINDINGS.md) and [`aj-script`](/Users/chotchki/workspace/guacamole-research/aj-script/FINDINGS.md):

- **The original's first fight is four Chompies bullying Norticus in a pit AG's walk can't reach.** They stand on the pit floor 70-75 m across the ground from the spawn and 21 m below it, past the ledge route's barrels and pot, the upper walkway, a 9 m drop and three push blocks ([`reach3.txt`](/Users/chotchki/workspace/guacamole-research/ah-propose/out/reach3.txt)). AG's 703 m² clearing holds no enemy and no experience; its gold is probably in the breakables (the level's drop rates read as the loot defaults, a hypothesis until the oracle breaks one).
- **Every combat rule is a script body, so AH runs the original's scripts.** The hurt chain, the Chompy's AI, Eruptor's attacks, experience orbs, loot, breakables, defeat and the figure's saves are tfbScript; the native share is comparisons, `random(N)` and movement. chotchki's answer to AG's Open question 2 (`f772507`) decides it: a stand-in "that would need a script body's logic rewritten ... stops there and AJ's VM slice is pulled forward instead". So AH carries the VM's core and the engine members the fight reaches, and AJ becomes the rest of the level's scripts on it. Statically, Chompy and Eruptor alone reach 193 of the 414 members the disc uses, every branch counted ([`reach_members.txt`](/Users/chotchki/workspace/guacamole-research/ah-propose/out/reach_members.txt)), about 60 of them sprites, particles, sounds, lights and strings; the whole whitelist reaches more, which AH.12 counts before any member box.
- **The numbers are small and known.** At medium, a chapter 1 Chompy has 1 health, bites for 11-14 and gives 4 experience as four 1-point orbs and no gold; Eruptor at level 1 has 290 health, and Lava Lob and each Eruption tick do 8. Level 2 is 1,000 experience, so no kid levels up in this fight.
- **The oracle's anchor plays medium** (AF.8's state reads the medium numbers live, [`live_values_l213_af8.tsv`](/Users/chotchki/workspace/guacamole-research/ah-propose/out/live_values_l213_af8.tsv)), and AF left the event and totals floors for the first rung that observes events: this one.

## What Changes

- **The fight is the original's**: the four Chompies around Norticus on the pit floor below the walkway, reached the original's way from the spawn. Five slices, each standing on less than the next: the phone keeps and edits a figure's progress; Eruptor throws on the TV (his attack scripts on the VM, the ball bursting on the clearing's floor); the fight through a dev-only entry at the pit (`test-hooks` and `dev-tools` builds) that fires the route's triggers in the original's order, for chotchki's first look and the oracle comparison; Eruptor smashes the clearing's breakables for their gold, which opens the ledge route; the route itself, every step from its scripts (Open question 1: does it gate M3).
- **The original scripts run**: the tfbScript VM's core, pulled forward from AJ: a new pure crate interpreting the op graphs `guac-formats` reads from the user's disc, with a per-tick op budget, a play mode that stops one script and logs it rather than panicking, and a fuzz target over the interpreter itself. A whitelist of the scripts the fight runs (the level's and the global init, Eruptor's, Setup_001's controller and four Chompies, Norticus where the fight reaches him, the breakables, coins and loot in reach, the route's triggers and push blocks, and what they spawn), the engine members and world operations they reach written root by root and counted against a ceiling recounted over every root, cosmetic members as plain values nothing draws, and one thin native rule for the portal manager's per-player loop. Eruptor's speed, turn rate and clip get one writer: where his scripts run, they drive AG's controller and AG's stand-ins step aside. AH writes the random generator down (MT19937 with its 1998 seeding in the design, the original's `random(N)` ranges as a spec, their inclusiveness observed at a boundary), the job the design doc gave AJ.
- **The figure store**: one row per profile per figure in guacamole's own database (experience as a total, gold, playtime, a generation), fresh on a profile's first placement; the TV hands the server the latest values, written at once and flushed on removal, level end and quit, so nothing earned is lost or comes back after an edit or reset; the figure's data reaches the scripts as a buffer in the layout the disc's own save-param table describes, never a file.
- **The phone's figure editor moves onto the rows**: guacamole's arms of the stats, edit and reset routes and the portal slot's reset (refused for the hero in a level), a check that the path's profile is the phone's unlocked one, the game's own level cap (15 in Giants) under guacamole and live updates to every phone on level and gold.
- **What the kids see on the TV**: the hero's health, level, experience and gold in a thin stand-in HUD in AP's theme, and the original's defeat (the figure out for the level, nothing lost) with a one-button restart screen when no character is left. The portal cards stay identity only.
- **The oracle grows**: readers for script variables, the level's enemy stats, the figure's experience and gold, difficulty, the experience multiplier, the element area, the loot index and the triggers fired; O6, a game-agnostic recorder for actors spawned mid-stretch, filtered and following pointer hops so its budgets hold; the fight's metrics on game time, one event log per actor, with horizons measured from the MT's draws; a route driver that walks the oracle to savestate anchors at the ledge route and the pit; and the event, totals and distribution floors AF left unset, calibrated by AF.12's method, with perturbations each checked visible.
- **The extract grows by parts**, AG.15's per-part rule: every animation palette slot of every character and of the creatures, the attack, orb and coin models the disc has, the progression tables and the whitelist's script data, with no layout bump.
- Out: upgrades, Magma Ball and Wow Pow (AK), Titan feats, particles, combat sound and the original's HUD (backlog lines, added now), the pit's other encounters and the rest of the level's scripts (AJ), menus (AM), co-op (AL), PvP (AI), Windows (the bring-up line).

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `guacamole/figure-progress`: a figure's progress per profile in guacamole's own store: fresh on first placement, earned in play, levels by the original's table, shown, edited and reset on the phone.
- `guacamole/combat`: how a hit lands for heroes and creatures alike: damage and criticals, armour deflects, no grace after a hit, stun and knockback, health, defeat.
- `guacamole/hero-attacks`: what each hero's attacks do under the buttons (Eruptor's two first) and which characters attack.
- `guacamole/creatures`: the creatures a level's scripts move and fight with, chapter 1's first fight first: waking, warning each other, closing in, biting and dying; what the fight wakes past its own.
- `guacamole/pickups`: experience orbs and coins, how a hero collects them, what each adds and levelling up.
- `guacamole/breakables`: what breaks under attacks, what it drops and leaves, the ways it opens, and what stays put for a Giant's feat.
- `guacamole/tv-hud`: the hero's numbers over the level and the screen when no character is left, guacamole's own until the original's interface runs.
- `guacamole/script-runtime`: the game's own computed values and random rolls coming out as the original's, and the difficulty played.

### Modified Capabilities

- `guacamole/figure-cards` (AA's requirements, unarchived): "Figure progress is not shown or edited yet" is REMOVED, its place taken by figure-progress.
- `guacamole/pad-input` (AG's new capability, unarchived): one ADDED requirement, the attack buttons.

The figure-cards and pad-input deltas sit on unarchived changes, so AH archives after AA and AG (design, Migration Plan).
