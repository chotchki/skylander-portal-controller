## AS. Every level through the extractor, and what their scripts need

Disc tests are `#[ignore]`d and read `GUAC_GIANTS_DUMP`; an extract a test writes goes under `GUAC_EXTRACT_DIR`, one level at a time, removed after, and none starts with under 40 GiB free.
