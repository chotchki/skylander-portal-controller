# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Level 205's music follows its own scripts
Level 205 SHALL play its music and ambience as its own sound scripts start them: the intro's track and the night's ambience at its start, the gameplay intro once the intro hands over, the same track kept through the first yard's fight, the thin explore bed once the yard is beaten, and a house's inside bed and ambience while a hero is inside it.

#### Scenario: The first yard
- **WHEN** a hero comes to level 205's first yard and beats its five
- **THEN** the music playing as he came keeps on through the fight, and the thin explore bed starts as the last foe goes

#### Scenario: Into a house
- **WHEN** a hero walks through the door of the house by the first yard
- **THEN** the inside bed and the house's ambience start as he comes into its room
