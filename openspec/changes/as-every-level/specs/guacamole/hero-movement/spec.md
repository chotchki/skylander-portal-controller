# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A wall leaning back is not ground
A surface facing up by less than 0.3 (about 72.5 degrees off straight up) SHALL NOT count toward the ground a hero stands on, so a railing he rubs does not tip the stairs' slope past 45 degrees and take the ground from a wide hero.

#### Scenario: A Giant up the hub's stairs
- **WHEN** Tree Rex holds the stick up the stairs to Flynn from any line across them
- **THEN** he walks up to Flynn's deck and the ground stays his
