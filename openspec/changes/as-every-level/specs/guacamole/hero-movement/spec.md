# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A wall leaning back is not ground
A face leaning back past 72.5° off up that a hero touches (a railing, a cliff face) SHALL NOT tip the ground under him past 45°, nor take away ground it makes with another face: beside walkable ground the original held him on 140 of 143 such recorded frames, and Tree Rex SHALL walk up the hub's stairs to Flynn from every line across them, where with the railing counted he slid back down on 6 of 11.

#### Scenario: A Giant up the hub's stairs
- **WHEN** Tree Rex holds the stick up the stairs to Flynn from any line across them
- **THEN** he walks up to Flynn's deck with the ground his all the way up, and there Flynn's talk answers

#### Scenario: Ground beside a cliff face
- **WHEN** a hero stands on walkable ground with a cliff face leaning back past 72.5° touching him
- **THEN** the ground holds him, as the original's held him on 14 of the 16 such moments in its recorded falls by the ledge route's cliff

### Requirement: A hero too big for his place comes in where a walk reaches
A hero coming in where the last stood who doesn't fit there SHALL be set at the nearest place within 30 m where he fits that a walk from there reaches, else at the newest point of the last hero's trail he fits that a walk back along it still reaches, never past a gap, a ledge or a closed door, gate or wall; with none, he SHALL stay. Of 6,761 swaps of Tree Rex for a small hero none SHALL set him where no walk reaches.

#### Scenario: A Giant swapped in behind a standing door
- **WHEN** Eruptor stands in chapter 1's fairy alcove behind its breakable door, the door standing, and Tree Rex is placed in his stead
- **THEN** Tree Rex comes in where Eruptor stood, on the alcove's side of the door

#### Scenario: A Giant swapped in where he fits a few metres on
- **WHEN** Eruptor stands between the pots and barrels at chapter 1's far end, where Tree Rex fits nowhere within 10 ft, and Tree Rex is placed in his stead
- **THEN** Tree Rex comes in 8 m along on the same ground and walks off with the stick on every heading
