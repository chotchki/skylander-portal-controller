# Phase AE - M1: the placed figure turns on the TV as its own animated model, remastered, named off the disc

## Why

M1 is the first 3D the kids see: put a figure on the phone and the TV shows that character, textured and idling, turning on its card. It stands on AC's decode gate, which PASSED on 2026-10-18 (AC.20; [`docs/native/guacamole-design.md`](../../../docs/native/guacamole-design.md), Why Giants first, What flips it): decoded from the disc alone, Eruptor matches what the original fed RSX on textures, geometry, skeleton and clip. What was missing for a turntable was the look and the list, and the 2026-10-18 research closed both:

- The look is ONE effect. [`ae-materials`](/Users/chotchki/workspace/guacamole-research/ae-materials/FINDINGS.md) found `tfbmulti2.fx` drawing 2,201 of the 2,243 character materials, its 9,216 RSX variants on the disc; [`ae-shader`](/Users/chotchki/workspace/guacamole-research/ae-shader/FINDINGS.md) decoded it into one function of 14 feature bits that reproduces all 4,096 pixel and 5,120 vertex programs, confirmed on 10,877 patched copies in a savestate. No sRGB anywhere: the game does its arithmetic on stored (gamma-encoded) values.
- RPCS3's Giants looks oversaturated, and that's the game, not the Mac ([`ae-color`](/Users/chotchki/workspace/guacamole-research/ae-color/FINDINGS.md), refuted and corrected in [`VERIFY.md`](/Users/chotchki/workspace/guacamole-research/ae-color/VERIFY.md)): the body variant adds colour map, difference lighting, spec and rim in gamma space, red goes past 1 on 44.9% of Eruptor's pixels, and the 8-bit store clamps each channel on its own, so orange lava flattens to yellow (107° where the unclamped colour is 80.5°). Stock Bevy only looked calmer because it wasn't drawing the game's material.
- The list is on the disc too ([`ae-roster`](/Users/chotchki/workspace/guacamole-research/ae-roster/FINDINGS.md)): 123 figures, names in 12 languages and a 256x256 portrait each, with all 77 rows of AA's interim wiki catalogue among them (12 under the wrong variant id). A research VM runs the same toy table and gets the same 123 ([`aj-vm`](/Users/chotchki/workspace/guacamole-research/aj-vm/FINDINGS.md)).

## What Changes

- **The disc roster replaces AA's interim catalogue.** guac-extract reads the toy table out of `character/init_setup.bld`, the names out of `permanent/global.bld`'s 12 language packs and the portraits out of `misc/ui_collection_champions.bld`; the server's figure list comes from that, and `guacamole` reads no wiki data after it ([`native_catalogue.rs`](../../../crates/guac-server/src/native_catalogue.rs) and its hand-written supplement go, and the game picker's box art comes off the dump's own `ICON0.PNG` instead of `data/games/`, chotchki 2026-10-18: "agreed"; the files themselves leave at G1, [`openspec/backlog.md`](../../backlog.md) Removals). The roster is read statically now and that reader leaves at AJ ("that's fine"), names are English with the other 11 languages kept in the IR ("agreed"), and without a recognized dump the phone lists no figures and says why ("agreed"). His two earlier calls (2026-10-18), both carried as is:
  - figure ids take the bits Giants itself reads from the tag's subtype word ("agreed"): alt deco 0x400 | deco id, Series 2 0x800, LightCore 0x200. Today's `0x0BFF` identity mask drops bit 10, Giants' "is legendary", so none of the 12 interim variant ids names its variant to the original scripts: by ae-roster's bit table five read as their character's Series 2 figure (Polar Whirlwind's 0x0802 is bit 11 on a toy in the Series 2 set; the disc's id is 0x0402), Legendary Chill's 0x0203 reads as LightCore Chill and the other six as the base. Guacamole's own tables get a migration for the 12.
  - Series 2 and LightCore are figures of their own with their own progress, though the disc treats each as its base character with a subtitle ("separate figures, we will now have access to ALL the figures so it will be easier to separate them all"). 123 entries, not 91.
- **One look, the remaster** (chotchki, 2026-10-18: "remastered is all I want to support, if I wanted faithful I'd do the emulator"):
  - The game's own material, textures and shading model (tfbmulti2's terms, written down in our words as the character-shading spec) in linear light without the clip to yellow: the disc's gamma-encoded colours go through `bevy_color` (`Srgba::rgb_u8` → `LinearRgba`), colour, spec and rim textures are sRGB-decoded, and the HDR result goes through TonyMcMapface ("let's do the modern bevy look, I'll decide later if there are lighting issues with it"). No exposure lift is tuned until he's looked (AE.18); the stock `StandardMaterial` render stays a dev-only comparison, `hero_view --stock`.
  - No look closer to the original's: no gamma-space arithmetic, 8-bit clamp, look setting or key. The oracle checks decoding and gameplay, not the look; the remaster's FLIP against an oracle frame is recorded, not gated (AE.16), and AC.18 closes on it ("ok").
  - One WESL shader (Bevy 0.20 replaced its WGSL dialect with WESL), its `@if` conditionals mapped onto tfbmulti2's feature bits, so a material's variant is a set of shader defs instead of ported microcode. A CPU model of the spec, from the spec text, checks it term by term, since no oracle frame does any more.
- **The turntable.** Each placed character's card shows its model off the disc: skinned with the bind AC.17 settled, posed by its own idle clip, its own materials, turning at a steady rate under the game's own 3D-UI light rig off `global.bld` ("agreed"). The model sits on each card, AA's two-by-four layout kept ("doesn't matter its just temporary"). Figures without a character model (items, adventure packs and sidekicks in AE) keep their disc portrait ("that's fine"). Every material the roster draws is compiled before the portal shows, because macOS compiles a pipeline inside the frame that first needs it.
- **The renderer floor on the Mac, measured**: CSM + SSAO + TAA + bloom at the TV's resolution on the M3 Max, GPU timings per pass, no temporal upscaler, pipelines pre-warmed. That number is the budget the Enhanced line ([`openspec/backlog.md`](../../backlog.md), beside the ladder) has to fit until the Windows/HTPC bring-up.
- **The extract grows**: the roster, portraits, each character model (geometry, skin, skeleton, materials, idle clip) and its KTX2 textures, with the roster as the FIRST stage so the phone's list lands seconds into a first run, and nothing but the intros waiting on ffmpeg. Models go into guac-ir, not glTF, by chotchki's rule ("whatever is easiest to load by bevy"; design decision 6 has the evidence).

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `guacamole/figure-roster`: the figures guacamole offers come off the user's own disc. Which figures, how they're named, elements and portraits, Series 2 and LightCore as figures of their own, no wiki data, what the phone shows before the roster is read, and figures saved under the interim ids coming back as the same figures.
- `guacamole/figure-turntable`: what the TV shows for a figure on the portal once it has a model: the character itself, textured, idling and turning on its card, variants as their own models, a fresh figure drawn as fresh, the portrait as the fallback, and never a stall.
- `guacamole/character-shading`: how guacamole shades a character, the original game's shading model written in our own words as the oracle shows it (the Method's spec for the shading model): the material, the lighting, specular, rim, tint, alpha and glow terms, and the quirks the look depends on.
- `guacamole/remaster-look`: the one look: that model in linear light at high dynamic range, tonemapped, which colours get decoded, the lava staying orange, and no look closer to the original's.

### Modified Capabilities

- `guacamole/figure-cards` (AA's delta, not archived yet): "Each figure on the portal shows as a card" carries the figure's turning model where its portrait was, the portrait staying as the fallback.
- `guacamole/disc-extraction` (AB's delta, not archived yet): "Only the original Giants disc is extracted" and "An unrecognized dump is reported, not guessed at" stop promising figure cards without a recognized dump (no interim catalogue is left to fall back on), and "Extraction runs once and survives interruption" puts the roster first so placing figures works seconds into a first run.

Both bases live in open changes, so `openspec validate` passes now and `openspec archive` refuses AE until AA and AB have archived (design, Migration Plan).
