**Ladder** (the rungs after AA/AB/AC, in order. Ids are provisional: `openspec/config.yaml` allocates the real one at `/opsx:propose`, so a product change proposed first shifts them (design doc, Decisions). That has happened once: the ACR122 removal took AD (`openspec/changes/ad-remove-acr122/`), so every rung after AC moved up a letter. The milestone names are the stable handles.)
- **AE (M1): animated turntable** — the placed figure as its textured, animated model turning on the TV. Proves the first 3D, the HTPC floor measured (CSM + SSAO + TAA + bloom at 4K on the 2080 Ti, DLSS on Vulkan) and the disc roster (names, elements, portraits) replacing AA's interim wiki catalogue, after which `guacamole` reads no wiki data. Stands on AC's PASS.
- **AF: oracle foundation** — nothing on the TV. Proves the oracle can be a test: on the pin AC.2 settled (the in-tree `accfecd2d` or the `09d602fd5` fallback), RPCS3 run-to-run divergence measured (10 replays from one savestate, Mac + HTPC), a dev-only env-gated input series (frame-locked analog sticks, two pads, press-at-frame-N, RNG peek + seed), tolerance metrics. Stands on no rung; lands before AG, the first gameplay comparison.
- **AG (M2): walk one level** — level scene, collision, character controller and camera, feel checked against the oracle; the input spec (positional mapping, prompts that match the pad in the player's hands). Stands on AE + AF. Physics: Avian ("probably avian if I had to guess", chotchki), behind a thin internal seam; until Avian releases for Bevy 0.20, the developer-recommended avianphysics/avian#1070 (Bevy 0.20 + Parry 0.31) as a git dependency pinned by rev, then the crates.io release. - **AH (M3): first fight** — one enemy, XP and gold earned and kept on the kid's profile. Proves combat basics and Guacamole's per-profile figure store (one instance per profile per figure, fresh on first placement), with the phone's figure editor moved onto its rows. Its tables are migrations in `guacamole`'s own database (the one AA starts under its own data root), never the controller's `db.sqlite`. Stands on AG.
- **AI (M4): first couch battle** — one Giants PvP arena, two pads, two phones. Proves a second pad and a second profile's figure in play, and the battle rules as the oracle shows them (any figure progress a battle awards goes to whoever placed the figure). Stands on AH plus AF's two-pad input.
- **AJ (M5): the level comes alive** — the tfbScript VM and its built-ins running the original script data from the user's IGZ (fixes go in the VM, never in the data). Stands on AG's level and AH's combat; the script-data census below comes first.
- **AK (M6): upgrades** — the upgrade screen; upgrades and path kept in the store per profile, movesets from script data. Stands on AJ.
- **AL (M7): co-op** — two pads and two figures in the adventure. Proves the second player on the host's story, the one shared camera and each kid's figure progress landing on their own profile. Stands on AI and AK.
- **AM (M8): chapter one** — a run of levels with story and cutscenes. Proves profile-owned story saves (one per profile per game, only the host's advances, no slot picker) and per-profile achievements by the co-op rules: a story one follows the host's save, a figure one the profile that placed the figure, and a counter both kids feed counts each kid's own share toward their own profile where Guacamole can tell whose share it was ("I'm good with that if we can detect it" — chotchki), otherwise only the host earns it. Stands on AL; the TROPDIR check below comes first.
- **AN (M9 = G1): the whole game** — all of Giants in `guacamole`, which becomes the only thing this repo builds and ships. The product binary leaves what ships with everything RPCS3 (P1-P8 as a product series, `rpcs3-control`'s IPC + UIA drivers, nested-app bundling and signing, `SetWindowPos` tiling, the CALayerHost embed), `crates/sky-parser` and the wiki data (Removals); the TV launcher surface (the QR badge) is rebuilt in Bevy. Nothing gets uninstalled: the controller on the HTPC keeps the other five games until Guacamole covers them, which AN's packaging has to guarantee (the MSI upgrades in place, so a `guacamole`-only MSI under the same upgrade code would remove the controller; design doc, G1). Stands on AM.
- **AO (G2): parity** — the oracle retires: the RPCS3 submodule and its dev-only patch series leave the repo. Stands on AN.
