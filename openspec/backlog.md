- **e2e `regressions` is timing-fragile on a warm build** (found by AD.4, 2026-10-17): its six tests spawn six servers + six headless Chromes at once, and each waits only 10s for `.portal-p4` while all six load the 56 MB debug phone WASM; on a warm tree all six fail, serially all six pass. Options: a longer `wait_for_portal` in that suite, a per-binary semaphore on `TestServer::spawn`, or `--test-threads` in the documented e2e command. Serial isn't immune either: on 2026-10-18 three of the six lost `.portal-p4` while a workspace build shared the CPU (6 of 6 alone).
